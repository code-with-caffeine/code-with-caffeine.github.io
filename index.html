  • Knee MRI report structuring

    Radiologists dictate reports as free text, which makes their findings hard to analyse across many patients. I fine-tuned a small open-source language model (0.5 billion parameters, using QLoRA) to turn knee MRI reports into structured JSON, covering the type, location and severity of findings, along with a plain-language summary. In test runs it approached 94% accuracy compared with frontier models, and it could cut the cost of the task by up to 99%.

    This is a research prototype trained on synthetic data. Validation, scaling and reliability testing are still ahead.

Writing

Atheria

A nuclear war caused the extinction of all life supporting mechanisms on the crust and the people carved out and snuggled beneath the land, creating intricate yet extensive systems to store, manage and distribute the energy to what's left of the civilisation. Now, the system falters and causes unexpected surges of energy and total cut-off often.

Amidst this Vats, a normal boy is learning the ways of society and quietly making his way into the remnants of bits of knowledge stored in the Grand Archive. Will he succeed in distilling all the knowledge and finding out a way to save their world, despite the incessant resistance from the ossified society?
