Work

Selected projects

Showing everything. Use the stars at the top to filter.

  • Knee MRI report structuring

    Radiologists dictate reports as free text, which makes their findings hard to analyse across many patients. I fine-tuned a small open-source language model (0.5 billion parameters, using QLoRA) to turn knee MRI reports into structured JSON, covering the type, location and severity of findings, along with a plain-language summary. In test runs it approached 94% accuracy compared with frontier models, and it could cut the cost of the task by up to 99%.

    This is a research prototype trained on synthetic data. Validation, scaling and reliability testing are still ahead.

    Model on Hugging Face

Atheria

A nuclear war has ended everything that kept the surface alive. The survivors live underground, in cities that depend on a vast network built to store and distribute energy. Now that network is failing, with sudden surges and blackouts.

In the Vats, an ordinary boy is learning how his society works. He is also slowly making his way into the Grand Archive, which holds what remains of the old world's knowledge. The question is whether he can piece it together and find a way to save his world, while a rigid society pushes back.
